# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in this
project a respectful and harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful and constructive
- Giving and accepting feedback gracefully
- Keeping discussion focused on project goals, evidence, and shared quality

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Publishing private information without permission
- Disruptive, hostile, or bad-faith interactions

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these
standards.

If you encounter unacceptable behavior, please report it through the contact
path described in [`SECURITY.md`](./SECURITY.md).
